Two college sweethearts on a mission to spread love 💕

Hey there! We're CupidCode! 👋

We're two college sweethearts who love coding as much as we love each other (okay, maybe not THAT much but close! 😂). Between classes and study sessions, we realized we could use our dev skills to help others express their feelings in creative ways!

Every page we create helps fund our tuition — so when you order from us, you're not just getting an amazing love page, you're also helping two students achieve their dreams! 🎓✨

We put our whole hearts into every design because we believe love deserves to be celebrated in the most creative way possible. Let us code your love story! 💖
